KoopTasks

A personal task list for one person, with a text-message front door.

Last updated 22 September 2026

SMS opt-in Messages Terms Privacy

KoopTasks is a small desktop app. Its list lives in the owner's own account, and the same account can be reached by text message: you text a task to the number and it is added to your list, and you can ask to be reminded about something at a time you choose. Messages only ever go to the phone number of the person who owns the account, and only because that person asked for them.

A standalone copy of the SMS opt-in instructions: SMS opt-in details

1. Text the keyword

Text SUBSCRIBE to (906) 767-6773. (START, UNSTOP and YES also work.)

2. You receive the welcome message

KoopTasks: you're subscribed. Text me things to do and I'll add them to your list, and I'll text you reminders you schedule. Msg frequency varies. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel. Terms & privacy: https://kylan02.github.io/tasks-sms/ To link your account: open the KoopTasks app, Settings, link, and text me the 6-digit code it shows.

3. Link the number to your account

Open the KoopTasks app, go to Settings, press link, and text the one-time 6-digit code it shows. The code belongs to that account alone and expires after 10 minutes. You then receive:

Linked! Text me anything you need to get done and I'll add it. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Until a number has texted the keyword, the only thing it can receive is this:

KoopTasks: text SUBSCRIBE to this number to start. Reply HELP for more.

Opting out — STOP

Text STOP at any time (STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT and UNLINK also work). Consent and the account link are both deleted immediately, and no further messages are sent:

KoopTasks: you're unsubscribed and your number is unlinked. No more messages will be sent. Text SUBSCRIBE to start again.

What gets sent, and how often

Every message is either a direct reply to something you texted, or a reminder you asked for at a time you chose. Nothing is promotional, and nothing is sent on anyone else's behalf. A typical day is between zero and ten messages.

  • You may use it only for your own task list, from a phone number you control.

Privacy policy

What is collected

  • Your phone number, once you complete the opt-in above, so replies and reminders can reach you.
  • The text of messages you send to the number, and the tasks and reminders created from them.
  • Your account identifier from signing in to the app with Google, and your email address as part of that sign-in.
  • A short, rolling record of recent messages on the number, so a follow-up like "put that one in Personal instead" makes sense in context.

How it is used

Only to run the service: to work out what you asked for, to keep your task list, and to send the replies and reminders you asked for. The text of your messages is sent to Google's Gemini API to be turned into tasks. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising or shared with anyone beyond the providers needed to deliver it — Twilio for messaging, Google Cloud and Firebase for storage and processing.

Where it is kept, and for how long

In Google Firebase, under the account it belongs to. Tasks and their history are kept until you delete them. The link between your phone number and your account is kept until you text STOP, which deletes it immediately.

Your choices

  • Text STOP to unlink your number and stop all messages.
  • Delete tasks in the app at any time.
  • Ask for your data to be deleted, or ask what is held, using the contact below.
